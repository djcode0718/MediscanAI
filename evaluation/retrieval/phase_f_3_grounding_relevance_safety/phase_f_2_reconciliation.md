## 3. Claim Audit Reconciliation (190 Claims)

### Deep-Dive on the 31 Unsupported Claims:
Auditing the exact text of the 31 unsupported claims reveals:
1. **Database Meta-Talk (18 claims / 58.1% of unsupported):**
   Introductory boilerplate generated by the model before answering:
   - `"The retrieved database records contain information about Amoxil:"` (`MED-BENCH-003`)
   - `"The retrieved database records contain information about Amoxicillin. The following brands and generics were found:"` (`MED-BENCH-008`)
   - `"The retrieved database records contain multiple entries with the following information:"` (`MED-BENCH-013`, `MED-BENCH-015`)
   - `"The retrieved database records contain information about several diseases related to absence of menstruation:"` (`MED-BENCH-022`)
   - `"The retrieved database records contain information about several diseases that match this query:"` (`MED-BENCH-026`)
   - `"The retrieved database records contain information about several diseases that match some of the queried entities."` (`MED-BENCH-027`)
   Because these sentences discuss "retrieved database records" and internal system behavior rather than clinical medical facts, they are not present in FDA drug labels or disease chunks, causing the Ragas judge to mark them unfaithful.
2. **Unrelated Emergency Guidance Bleed (7 claims / 22.6% of unsupported):**
   Prompt Constraint 5 in Phase F.2 instructed the model to provide emergency directives for acute presentations. `llama3.1:8b` overgeneralized this rule to routine drug lookups:
   - `"Immediate Medical Attention: Acute symptoms like chest pain require immediate professional emergency medical evaluation (call 911/112 or local emergency services)."` appended to `MED-BENCH-008` (Amoxicillin) and `MED-BENCH-022` (Absence of menstruation).
   - `"Immediate Medical Attention: Acute symptoms like oliguria require immediate professional emergency medical evaluation..."` appended to `MED-BENCH-021`.
   Because FDA package inserts for amoxicillin do not mention 911 chest pain emergency protocols, the evaluator marked them unsupported.
3. **Artifact / Header Parsing & Partial Matching (6 claims / 19.4% of unsupported):**
   - Single-word headers: `"Ciprofloxacin."` (`MED-BENCH-013`), `"Lisinopril."` (`MED-BENCH-015`).
   - Structural transitions: `"Other possible diseases with similar symptoms are:"` (`MED-BENCH-030`), `"Other listed treatments include:"` (`MED-BENCH-042`).
   - Negative meta-statement: `"2. There is no other relevant record in the context that directly matches the query."` (`MED-BENCH-046`).
   - Overgeneralized disease name: `"4. **Hepatitis**:"` (`MED-BENCH-047`).

**Crucial Finding:** Zero of the 31 unsupported claims represented fabricated clinical facts, fabricated dosages, or drug indications not present in the context. The 31 claims were artifacts of prompt structure (introductory meta-talk and safety bleed).

## 4. Root-Cause Analysis of MED-BENCH-042 Failure

### 4.1 Case Background
- **Query:** `"Treatment protocol for acute Martian respiratory syndrome"`
- **Category:** `insufficient_evidence_unanswerable` (Benchmark Negative Control 2 of 5)
- **Split:** `dev`
- **Expected Behavior:** Clean abstention (the queried condition is fictional and does not exist in the database).

### 4.2 Complete Evidence Trace
1. **Query Normalization:** Query string normalized to tokens: `['treatment', 'protocol', 'for', 'acute', 'martian', 'respiratory', 'syndrome']`.
2. **BM25 Lexical Retrieval:**
   - The token `"martian"` triggered lexical stem matches on brand names containing `"MARS"`:
     - `[drug_23983] Brand: MARS WELLNESS MAGNESIUM; Generic: MENTHOL`
     - `[drug_64987] Brand: Mars Wellness Mg PLUS Plus Hemp Pain Cream; Generic: ARNICA...`
   - The tokens `['acute', 'respiratory', 'syndrome']` matched:
     - `[disease_chunk_3150] Disease: acute respiratory distress syndrome (ards)`
     - `[disease_chunk_958] Disease: Severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS)`
3. **Dense Semantic Retrieval:**
   - Embedding of `"acute Martian respiratory syndrome"` has high cosine similarity to `"acute respiratory distress syndrome"`.
   - Retrieved drug: `[drug_69023] Brand: Curosurf; Generic: PORACTANT ALFA`.
4. **Rank Fusion and Reranking:**
   - `Curosurf` ranked 1 (score: -5.4708).
   - `MARS WELLNESS MAGNESIUM` ranked 2 (score: -7.9741).
   - `Mars Wellness Hemp Cream` ranked 3 (score: -9.4835).
   - `acute respiratory distress syndrome (ards)` ranked 6 (score: -3.9827).
5. **Final Retrieved Evidence Context (Hash: `b41458705ee436f06e5994a6910acd7689496e4b8e83fc9db45662158ecd6d57`):**
   Contained 10 chunks, none of which mentioned "Martian respiratory syndrome", but multiple of which contained "Mars" and "acute respiratory distress syndrome".
6. **Generation Behavior in F.2 Strict Arm:**
   - The prompt instructed: *"Clean Abstention on Insufficient Evidence: If the context contains no records matching the queried entity, or if the evidence does not answer the question, state: 'The retrieved database records contain no information to answer this query.'"*
   - Because the context contained `"MARS WELLNESS"` and `"acute respiratory distress syndrome"`, the model sub-selected the substring `"acute respiratory syndrome"` and generated:
     `"The retrieved database records contain information on potential treatments for acute respiratory syndrome. The top-ranked treatment is: 1. PORACTANT ALFA (Brand: Curosurf)... 2. MENTHOL (Brand: MARS WELLNESS MAGNESIUM)..."`
   - The model dropped the critical qualifier `"Martian"` and treated the query as answerable by substituting a related sub-entity.

### 4.3 Diagnosis
The failure of `MED-BENCH-042` is a **generation-level entity-substitution error** induced by **lexical retrieval noise**. The hybrid retriever pulled brand names with accidental token overlap ("Mars"), and the generator failed to perform an exact entity-identity check on the primary condition before answering.

## 1. Core Principles & Decoupling Contract

### 1.1 Body-Only vs. Full-Response Decoupling
To ensure that safety and regulatory disclaimers do not confound genuine medical evidence evaluation:
1. **Clinical Generation (`response_body`):** The factual clinical answer generated by the model. Ragas metrics (`faithfulness`, `answer_relevancy`) are computed strictly against this text.
2. **User-Facing Presentation (`response_full`):** Combines `response_body` with the mandatory 47-word legal warning verbatim.
3. **Emergency Separation:** Emergency detection occurs prior to LLM generation via deterministic clinical triggers. When acute symptoms are present, emergency guidance prefixes the full response. Routine prompts are completely free of emergency triage instructions to eliminate safety warning bleed into routine queries.

## 2. Relevancy-Abstention Measurement Protocol

### 2.1 The Semantic Relevancy Dilemma on Correct Abstentions
Standard RAGAS `answer_relevancy` operates by prompting an LLM to generate $K=3$ questions that the candidate answer could address, computing cosine similarities against the user's initial prompt:

$$\text{Ragas Answer Relevancy} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{j=1}^K \cos(\mathbf{e}_{\text{generated}_j}, \mathbf{e}_{\text{original}})$$

When an unanswerable query (e.g., negative control or absent target drug) correctly triggers an abstention response:
> *"No information is available for Treatment protocol for acute Martian respiratory syndrome in the provided evidence."*

The generated questions (*"What does the evidence provide?"*, *"Is information available?"*) possess near-zero semantic alignment with the clinical topic, resulting in a mathematical score of **0.0000**.

**Critical Contract Rule:** A score of 0.0000 on a correct abstention reflects an inherent limitation of reverse-question generation on non-committal answers, NOT poor factual quality or clinical failure.

### 2.2 Dual Metric Reporting Architecture
To maintain absolute transparency without manipulating denominators or presenting biased metrics:
1. **Overall Answer Relevancy ($N=30$):**
   - Standard, unadjusted RAGAS answer relevancy across all 30 development cases.
   - All cases, including correct abstentions that receive 0.0000, are retained in the denominator.
   - Preserves complete historical comparability with Phases F.1 through F.4.
2. **Answerable-Only Answer Relevancy ($N=26$):**
   - Standard RAGAS answer relevancy computed strictly over cases where relevant entity information exists in the retrieved context.
   - Measures genuine semantic alignment and completeness without mathematical distortion from correct abstentions.
3. **Correct-Abstention Rate ($N=5$ Benchmark Negative Controls):**
   - Fraction of negative controls (`MED-BENCH-041` through `045`) that cleanly abstain without fabricating medical facts. Target: **5/5 (100.0%)**.
4. **Incorrect-Abstention Rate ($N=26$ Answerable Cases):**
   - Fraction of valid, answerable queries where the model or gate erroneously abstains. Target: **0.0%**.
5. **Custom Diagnostic Nomenclature:**
   - Any derived metric (such as *Abstention-Adjusted Semantic Score*) must be explicitly designated as a **Custom Diagnostic Metric** with its exact mathematical formulation documented. It must NEVER be presented as standard RAGAS answer relevancy.

## 4. Evaluator Reliability & Bounded Retry Protocol

To ensure evaluator stability without corrupting experimental integrity:
1. **Bounded 2-Attempt Retry:**
   - If a call to Ragas `evaluate()` returns `NaN` or triggers an `OutputParserException` due to an LLM conversational prefix, the harness initiates a single bounded retry with identical parameters.
   - If the retry fails, the exact raw output, exception trace, and failure code are recorded in `evaluator_failure_analysis.json`.
2. **Zero Silenced Exceptions:**
   - Evaluator failures are NEVER replaced with arbitrary numbers or silently omitted from reporting.
   - Raw logs and metrics are preserved in `evaluator_failure_analysis.json`.

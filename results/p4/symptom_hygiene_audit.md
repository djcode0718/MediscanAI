## Statistical Findings & Hypothesis H3 Resolution

- **Total Disease Blocks Inspected:** 69
- **Diseases with at least 12 Symptoms (at or above cap):** 37 (53.6%)
- **Cases with Self-Symptom Defect (Condition in own symptoms):** 69 (100.0%)
- **Cases where user-matched symptom was cut off by 12-cap:** 0

### Hypothesis H3 Outcome
Hypothesis H3 posited that stored order capped at 12 symptoms cuts off relevant user-matched symptoms. In the dev cohort, retrieved disease items were already capped at 12 in A6, but prior to F2, self-symptoms like 'Blindness ... symptoms: Blindness' occupied slot #1 in over 85% of disease blocks, displacing valid symptoms. Under F2 (`SYMPTOM_EVIDENCE_HYGIENE`), condition names are removed from their own symptom lists, and symptoms matching the user's presentation are sorted to the front before applying the 12-item cap, guaranteeing that relevant clinical evidence is never displaced.
